How it works

Your chart stays on your computer.

Deltalyn runs next to TradingView Desktop and talks to it directly. Nothing about your chart goes to our servers.

Step 1

Open TradingView Desktop

Use your own TradingView subscription and layouts. Deltalyn starts the app with a local connection and checks that it works.

Step 2

Ask in plain English

"Build my pre-market brief for NQ." Deltalyn reads price, indicators and the levels your Pine scripts draw.

Step 3

Approve what changes

Reading is automatic. Anything that changes your chart, such as a line or an alert, waits for your OK.

Workflows

Built around your trading day.

Most evaluations are lost on preparation and discipline. Deltalyn gives each part of the day a routine.

Before the open

Pre-market brief

Overnight range, prior-day high and low, key levels from your indicators, and the economic events on today's calendar. One screen, sources shown.

Practice

Replay coach

Pick a date and drill it bar by bar in TradingView's replay mode. Deltalyn asks why you took each simulated trade and notes where you broke your own rules.

After the close

Session review

A plain summary of what the market did against your plan, saved with screenshots to a private journal on your computer.

Also included: a Pine Script assistant that writes, compiles and fixes scripts in your editor, and a multi-symbol scan for your watchlist.

Every number has a source

It shows its work.

Values read straight from your chart are marked Observed. Anything the AI estimates is marked Inferred, so you always know which is which.

Trust

Designed to keep you in control.

Runs on your computer

Deltalyn connects to TradingView Desktop locally. Your chart data is not uploaded to us or resold.

You approve every change

Lines, alerts, symbol changes and Pine saves are shown to you first. Nothing is applied until you confirm.

Never places trades

No broker connection, no order buttons. Replay trades are simulated and stay in TradingView's replay mode.

No signals, no promises

Deltalyn describes the chart and helps you follow your own plan. It does not tell you what to buy or sell.

FAQ

Questions traders ask.

Is this a trading bot or a signal service?

No. Deltalyn reads your chart and helps you prepare, practice and review. It does not give buy or sell recommendations and it cannot place orders.

What do I need to use it?

TradingView Desktop on Windows or macOS, with your own TradingView plan. A paid plan is recommended for real-time data. Deltalyn uses Claude for its reasoning; during the beta you connect your own Claude account.

Does it work with my broker or prop firm platform?

Deltalyn works only with TradingView Desktop. It does not connect to brokers or prop firm platforms, so it never touches your account or your orders.

Is Deltalyn made by TradingView?

No. Deltalyn is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with TradingView, Inc.

What happens when TradingView updates their app?

Updates can change how the desktop app works inside. We test against new versions and publish which versions are supported. If something breaks, Deltalyn tells you instead of showing stale data.

How much will it cost?

The beta is free. After launch we plan a monthly subscription, and waitlist members get first access to a limited founding-member license.

¿Habrá versión en español?

Sí. Después del lanzamiento en Estados Unidos llegará la versión en español para Latinoamérica y España. Únete a la lista y te avisamos.
